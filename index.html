    25+years of printing for brands

    A complete label house, from artwork to finished rolls.

    Shivay Screen Printer, known to many customers as SSP Labels, works from its own unit on the Sahnewal–Dehlon Road in Ludhiana. The name comes from screen printing. Today the floor runs offset, flexo and screen printing side by side.

    Each process is good at different things, so we choose the one that suits your label instead of forcing every job through the same machine. When a label needs more than one, we combine them.

    From your idea to a finished label

    Seven steps, the same for every order. Watch the label take shape as you scroll.

    Tell us what you needStep 1 of 7
    1. 1

      Tell us what you need

      Share the product, label size, quantity and container on WhatsApp or a call. A photo of your bottle or pack helps us suggest the right material.

    2. 2

      Artwork and print check

      Send your design and we check it for print: colours, bleed, die-line and text size. If you need help preparing it, we work on it with you.

    3. 3

      Proof and approval

      You approve a proof before anything goes to press. Nothing is printed without your go-ahead.

    4. 4

      Plates, screens and dies

      Your artwork is separated into colours and we make the plates, screens and cutting dies the job needs.

    5. 5

      Printing

      The colours print in register on offset, flexo or screen, or a mix of them, checked against the approved proof.

    6. 6

      Finishing

      Lamination, varnish, foil, spot UV, embossing, die-cutting and slitting into rolls or sheets.

    7. 7

      Quality check and dispatch

      Labels are inspected, counted, packed and dispatched from our unit in Ludhiana.
